Description (required)
────────────────────────────────────────
Porterchain Customer is how you request capacity on Porterchain’s Transportation Capacity Network.

You can:

• Get a quote for a pickup and drop-off
• Book and pay in Stripe Checkout
• Track a shipment, including without an account
• See orders, bookings, parcel details, and billing
• Report a problem and export or request deletion of your data

Sign-in uses Porterchain Platform. The amount on the quote is the Porterchain price. This app does not set its own rates.

Porterchain is not a consumer courier, a trucking company, or marketplace software. This app is the customer side of the capacity network.

Notes (paste as one block)
────────────────────────────────────────
Porterchain Customer (ASC name: PCDC, bundle com.porterchain.customer) is the customer app for Porterchain’s Transportation Capacity Network.

Sign-in is PorterChain Platform (clerk.admin.porterchain.com), not the driver Clerk app. Tap Sign in and use ravichauhan7434@gmail.com with the password in this form. Create account is public self sign-up. Sign out is not shown on the empty form. It is at the bottom of Account and asks for confirmation.

Without an account, tap “Track a shipment without an account”. An unknown number says “Shipment not found.”

After sign-in, Home shows recent shipments. Book requests a quote. The price is returned by Porterchain. Pay opens Stripe Checkout in the browser. Activity has orders, bookings, parcels, and billing. Account has support, privacy export, and deletion request.

Push to the phone is off. Alerts still lists in-app updates. The app does not request location or camera. Face ID only unlocks a saved session.

Encryption: HTTPS only (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false).

Please do not reject for missing push, or because Stripe returns through the Porterchain website as well as the in-app check.
