App Review notes — Porterchain Customer (com.porterchain.customer, ASC PCDC)

What to test
  1. On the signed-out screen, confirm Sign in and Create account. Sign out is not on that empty form.
  2. Track a shipment without an account: “Track a shipment without an account”. An unknown number says “Shipment not found.”
  3. Sign in as ravichauhan7434@gmail.com (hosted Clerk on PorterChain Platform).
  4. Home opens only for a provisioned customer. Book a quote. The amount is returned by Porterchain. Pay opens Stripe Checkout in the browser.
  5. Activity shows orders, bookings, parcels, and billing. Account shows the profile, a support form, privacy export, deletion request, and Sign out at the bottom. Sign out asks for confirmation.

Sign-in
  PorterChain Platform at clerk.admin.porterchain.com. This is not the driver Clerk instance. Demo user: ravichauhan7434@gmail.com. That user must already be a customer. A driver or staff account stays on onboarding or Access denied and can sign out.

Push
  Shipment push to the phone is off in this build. Alerts says updates stay in the in-app list. That does not block quote, book, or track.

Face ID
  Optional unlock of a saved session. The review device can skip it.

Location and camera
  The app does not request device location or camera.

Encryption
  ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false (HTTPS only).

Account deletion
  Account → Request deletion. That opens a hold for review. Booking stops while the hold is open. Support is the form on the same screen, or porterchaininc@gmail.com.

Do not reject for
  • Push not registered on the review device
  • Payment completing on the Porterchain website after Stripe Checkout; the app also checks the booking when the browser closes
  • No map of live GPS in the app. Status and ETA come from Porterchain
