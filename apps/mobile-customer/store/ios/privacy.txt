App Store privacy nutrition — Porterchain Customer (PCDC)

Data used to provide the app (linked to identity, not used for tracking):

  Contact info
    Email and name from the Porterchain Platform account. Phone number entered on the booking form.

  User content
    Pickup and drop-off addresses, delivery instructions, support messages.

  Identifiers
    User ID from Clerk. This build does not register a push token.

  Purchases
    No in-app purchases. Card payment happens in Stripe Checkout in the browser. The app shows invoice and receipt links returned by Porterchain.

Not collected
  Precise device location, photos, advertising data, health, contacts, browsing history.
  Address suggestions, when a Maps key is configured, are typed addresses sent to Google Places. The app does not request the device location permission. Distance and price are calculated by Porterchain, not by Google.

Tracking
  No. No advertising SDK. App Tracking Transparency is not required.

Third parties
  Clerk (PorterChain Platform sign-in), Stripe (Checkout in the browser), Porterchain API.
  Google Places is optional and is used only for address autocomplete.
