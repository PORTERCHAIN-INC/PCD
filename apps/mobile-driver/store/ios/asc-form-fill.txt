Description (required)
────────────────────────────────────────
Porterchain Driver is the field app for drivers on Porterchain’s Transportation Capacity Network.

You get the work that is assigned to you — the next stop, the job number, wallet and today’s earnings — and you run the loop on the van:

• Start a shift only after a short pre-trip check
• Navigate to the stop and mark arrival when you are actually there
• Scan package QR or Code 128 labels that belong to this order
• Capture proof of delivery photos
• Keep licence, insurance, and vehicle documents current
• See jobs, money, and messages without a dispatch console on your phone

Location is used while you are on duty so dispatch can see the van, including with the screen locked. Camera is used for proof of delivery and label scans. Sign-in is for approved Porterchain drivers only.

Porterchain is not a consumer courier, a trucking company, or marketplace software. This app is the driver side of our capacity network.

Notes (App Review Information — paste as one block)
────────────────────────────────────────
Porterchain Driver (ASC name: PCD) is for approved Porterchain network drivers only. There is no public self-signup.

Sign in: tap “Sign in with Porterchain”, then use the User name / Password in this form (Clerk hosted auth).

After sign-in you should land on Work. Check Jobs, Money, Docs, and More — none require a live route. Work shows the assigned stop when a job is on the board; otherwise wallet and duty controls still appear.

Background location (5.1.4): precise location is sent only while the driver is On duty so dispatch (Fleetbase) can see the van with the screen locked. Off duty stops the on-duty ping. Not used for ads or tracking.

Camera: proof of delivery photos and package QR / Code 128 scans for this order. Microphone is not used.

Face ID: optional unlock of a saved session.

Push: APNs for job assigned / stop changes. Missing push registration on the review device does not block core flows.

Encryption: standard HTTPS only (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false).

Account deletion: email porterchaininc@gmail.com (drivers are ops-provisioned). In-app: More → Support.

Please do not reject for: no public registration; geofence “I’ve arrived” may refuse until the device pin is inside the stop.
