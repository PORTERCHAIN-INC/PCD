App Store privacy nutrition — Porterchain Driver

Data used to provide the app (linked to identity, not used for tracking):

  Contact info
    Email, name, phone — Clerk driver account and ops profile.

  Location
    Precise location — on-duty GPS pings to PorterChain dispatch so the van can be seen on the map.

  Photos
    Proof of delivery and document uploads (licence, insurance, vehicle). User-provided.

  User content
    Support messages, exception notes, pre-trip checks.

  Identifiers
    User ID (Clerk), device push token (APNs).

  Product interaction
    Job accept/complete, scan events, duty status.

Not collected
  Advertising data, browsing history, health, purchases (no IAP), other financial accounts.

Tracking
  No. No third-party advertising SDK. ATT not required.

Third parties
  Clerk (authentication), Apple (APNs, Map tap-out to Apple Maps), Porterchain API.
  Google Maps is not used as a routing engine in this app.
