App Review notes — Porterchain Driver (com.porterchain.PCD)

What to test
  1. Sign in with the App Review account (hosted Clerk: tap “Sign in with Porterchain”).
  2. Land on Work. Profile shows an approved driver.
  3. Open Today, Job and Me. None of these require a live route.
  4. Job shows the assigned stop when dispatch has given this driver a job. If no job is on the board, Job shows an empty state and Today shows the vehicle check and duty controls.

Sign-in
  Use the demo driver account supplied in App Store Connect (Notes field). This app is not a public consumer product. Only approved Porterchain drivers can complete handshake.

Background location (Guideline 5.1.4)
  NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription, NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription, and NSLocationAlwaysUsageDescription are set.
  UIBackgroundModes includes location.
  Precise location is used only while the driver is on duty so PorterChain dispatch can see the van with the screen locked. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. The driver can go off duty to stop the on-duty ping.

Camera
  Used for proof-of-delivery photos and scanning this-order package QR / Code 128 labels. Microphone is not requested.

Face ID
  Optional unlock of a saved Clerk session on this device. Simulator skips hardware.

Push
  APNs job-assigned and stop-change alerts. The review device may show Push as not registered; that does not block Work.

Encryption
  ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false (HTTPS only).

Account deletion
  Drivers are provisioned by Porterchain operations. To delete a review account, email porterchaininc@gmail.com. In-app, dispatch contact is under Me.

Do not reject for
  • No public sign-up (invite / ops-provisioned drivers only)
  • Simulator GPS / geofence “I've arrived” may stay honest-fail until the pin is inside the stop geofence
  • Expo / TestFlight vs Simulator: production binary has no developer Tools button
