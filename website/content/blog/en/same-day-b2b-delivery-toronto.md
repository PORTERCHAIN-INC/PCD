Same-day in Toronto is not a slogan. It is staged freight, a vehicle that fits the stop, and a driver who can hit a window in real traffic.

## Zone-realistic cut-offs

Downtown, Peel, and York are not one blanket SLA. Publish cut-offs by pickup zone so inside sales stops promising windows operations cannot hit.

## Vehicle class first

Parcels and totes need different capacity than long stock or pallets. Wrong class burns the window even when the driver is on time.

## Proof closes the day

Photo and signature POD stop the evening dispute cycle. Same-day without proof just moves the argument to tomorrow.
