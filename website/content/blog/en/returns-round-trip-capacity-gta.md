Returns fail when ops treat the second address as optional. In the GTA, equipment swaps, unsold stock pickups, and failed-delivery recovery need the same vehicle match and proof as outbound lanes.

## Two stops, one commercial promise

Book both legs with clear access notes. A van that fit the outbound cube must still fit the return — and the dock contact must be reachable.

## Capacity, not a rental SaaS app

You keep the customer relationship and inventory system. Capacity partners move freight between addresses with tracking and proof — they do not replace your merchandising stack.

## Close both ends

Photo and GPS on pickup and delivery close billing and inventory adjustments without chasing drivers.
