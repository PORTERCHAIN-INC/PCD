Multi-location operators rarely fail because they lack a map. They fail when freight is staged at the wrong branch, cut-offs differ by site, and status lives in three group chats.

## One capacity partner, many origins

Counters in Toronto and docks in Peel can share vehicle-and-driver capacity with the same tracking and proof standards. The quote should name the pickup origin — not assume every run leaves one hub.

## Transfers are deliveries too

Branch balancing needs the same discipline as customer drops: staging, vehicle class, and closeout evidence.

## Skip the SaaS trap

You do not need a new inventory platform to move staged freight. You need capacity that completes — and ops recovery when a window stalls.
