Most delivery disputes are memory contests: who was on site, what time, was the dock open. Claims-ready proof ends that contest.

## What good closeout includes

- Photo confirmation
- Receiver signature when required
- GPS timestamp at completion
- Exception reason when the stop fails

## Why it matters for trade lanes

Jobsite and wholesale receivers escalate fast. Shared evidence keeps GCs, counters, and finance aligned.

## Path when something is damaged

Start from the shipment record, not a disconnected email thread. Ops and enterprise contacts should work the same evidence chain.
