A transfer is a two-stop capacity problem: pull cleanly, deliver cleanly, prove it moved. Treat it like a customer SLA.

## Stage before you dispatch

SKU references, dock contact, and ready freight beat a hopeful same-day request every time.

## Match cube to vehicle

Cartons and totes are not pallets. Wrong class burns the window even when the driver is early.

## Close for accountability

Photo and GPS timestamps help retail and warehouse agree the stock arrived — useful when loss-prevention asks questions.
