PorterChain prices transportation capacity — not software seats. The useful decision is which shipping pattern you need this quarter.

## Overflow / same-day

Best when a driver is out, a truck is down, or demand spikes. You want backup capacity with the same tracking and proof standards as primary lanes.

## Recurring routes

Best for weekly café, wholesale, clinic, or warehouse loops. Fixed days and windows with overflow available when the template breaks.

## Dedicated program

Best when volume is steady enough to reserve coverage and name a dispatch relationship. Lane pricing replaces trip-by-trip roulette.

## Brief one quote, mix patterns later

Most GTA accounts mix overflow and recurring. Share corridors, vehicle class, and proof needs — get a written capacity path, not a monthly SaaS plan.
