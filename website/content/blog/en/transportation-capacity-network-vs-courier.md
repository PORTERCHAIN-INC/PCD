Ad-hoc couriers solve today's emergency and create tomorrow's inconsistency. A transportation capacity network sells completed runs: matched vehicles, professional drivers, ops recovery, and proof.

## The product is the completed stop

Software seats do not move freight. Capacity does. Technology coordinates; drivers and vehicles execute.

## Primary and overflow together

The same partner can cover recurring lanes and peak overflow so customers never feel the scramble.

## Evaluate coverage, not demos

Ask about GTA zones, vehicle classes, POD standards, and written quotes. That is the commercial test.
