Clinic loops fail when every stop becomes a call. Treat replenishment like a capacity program.

## Sequence the corridor

Group clinics by zone and window. Dense multi-stop beats a sedan bouncing across the GTA all afternoon.

## Proof for regulated receivers

Photo and GPS closeout help pharmacy and clinic ops answer compliance questions without chasing drivers.

## Recurring plus same-day

Lock the weekly template. Keep overflow for stockouts and missed cuts.
