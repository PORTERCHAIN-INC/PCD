Foodservice windows do not move for traffic. Roasters and wholesale food distributors need early capacity that arrives with tracking cafés and kitchens can trust.

## Recurring first, overflow second

Lock weekly café or restaurant loops so product leaves on schedule. Keep same-day overflow for promotions and missed cuts without inventing a new courier each time.

## Vehicle fit for perishable freight

Totes, cases, and mixed cartons need the right van class and staging discipline. Overpacking a sedan to “save” the quote usually costs the window.

## Proof that quiets the phone

Shareable ETA and photo closeout reduce “where is my drop?” calls during the breakfast rush.
