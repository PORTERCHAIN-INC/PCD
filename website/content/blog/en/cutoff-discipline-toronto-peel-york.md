Treating the GTA as one SLA is how sales overpromises and docks underdeliver.

## Zone before slogan

Downtown counters, Peel warehouses, and York jobsites do not share the same staging reality. Publish cut-offs by pickup zone.

## Stage before you quote urgency

Freight that is not ready by the cut-off is not a same-day problem — it is an intake problem. Capacity cannot invent time.

## Overflow when your fleet is full

Book backup capacity with the same tracking and proof so receivers still get a clean window.
