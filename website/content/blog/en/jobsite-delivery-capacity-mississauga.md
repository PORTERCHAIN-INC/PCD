Jobsite delivery fails on access notes and wrong trucks more often than on distance. Mississauga and Peel sites need capacity that fits the gate and the pallet.

## Capture the right contact

GC or site supervisor — not only the purchasing office. Failed access burns the window.

## Match truck to site

Box truck for pallets; trade van for long stock when the site is tight. Confirm class in the quote.

## Proof for billing

Photo POD closes GC disputes and keeps inside sales out of the evening chase.
