Peel docks move waves. When merchant SLAs are tight, 3PLs need outbound capacity that matches carton and pallet mix — plus tracking merchants can share.

## Outbound is a capacity product

Software alone does not clear a dock. You need vehicles, drivers, and cut-offs aligned to the wave.

## Multi-stop and peak together

Promotions and returns spikes should not force permanent headcount. Book overflow on the same standards as primary lanes.

## Merchant-visible status

Shareable tracking and POD keep brands confident without putting every ETA question on your account managers.
