Pharmacy delivery is urgency plus accountability. Patients and clinics need ETAs; ops need a record that holds up when something goes wrong.

## Same-day with a paper trail

Tracking and proof are not optional on regulated B2B runs. Status should live with the shipment.

## Recurring plus urgent

Clinic replenishment and patient exceptions can share one capacity partner so the team is not juggling apps.

## Start with zones and windows

Share typical lanes, handling notes (coolers for items that must stay cool), and GTA zones. Then price it in about 10 seconds on the calculator — no demo tour.
