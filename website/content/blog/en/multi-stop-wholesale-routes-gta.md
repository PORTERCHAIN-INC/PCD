Wholesale rarely ships one door. Counter-to-contractor and warehouse-to-store loops need route density, access notes, and closeout proof that finance will accept.

## Density beats isolated stops

Generic courier tools treat every stop as a one-off. Multi-stop wholesale needs sequenced loops across Peel and York with realistic ETAs.

## Stage before you promise

Late staging is the most common failure. Align cut-offs so freight is ready before the first wheel turns.

## One partner for overflow days

When the template breaks, overflow on the same partner keeps receivers from feeling courier roulette.
