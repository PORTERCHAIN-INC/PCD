Ship-from-store only works when the store can stage freight and capacity can hit the window. It is not a checkout widget by itself.

## Capacity from the branch that has stock

Match pickup to the location that can actually pull the order. Wrong store staging is the top failure mode.

## Customer promise vs ops reality

Whether the shopper sees “same-day” or “standard,” ops still needs vehicle class, cut-off, and proof.

## One partner across locations

Multi-location routing plus same-day overflow beats a different courier per branch.
