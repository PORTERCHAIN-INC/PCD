Peak days and breakdowns do not wait for hiring cycles. GTA wholesalers, construction suppliers, and warehouses miss cut-offs when their own fleet is already booked — then scramble for ad-hoc couriers with no proof standard.

## Overflow is capacity insurance

Treat overflow as a planned release valve: the same vehicle classes you already use, professional drivers, shareable tracking, and proof on every stop. Receivers should not feel a scramble when volume spikes.

## When teams book it

- Promotion and launch days
- A truck in the shop or a driver call-out
- Hard afternoon cut-offs that outrun your own routes

## How to start

Share typical peak patterns, vehicle class, and zones. A written quote beats phone roulette when the dock is already full.
