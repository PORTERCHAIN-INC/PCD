#### FAQ (must include these questions verbatim for SEO snippets)

1. **Do you offer same-day delivery in Toronto and the GTA?**  
   Yes. Same-day and urgent delivery are available across our GTA service areas subject to cut-off times, vehicle class, and capacity. Request a quote and we confirm the window.

2. **Can you handle emergency delivery when our driver is unavailable?**  
   Yes. Emergency delivery and fleet overflow are core services — we dispatch matched vehicle and driver capacity, often same day.

3. **What is intra-city logistics and do you provide it?**  
   Intra-city logistics is moving freight between locations within the GTA — warehouses, jobsites, stores, and clinics. We run single-stop, multi-stop, and recurring intra-city routes.

4. **Do you provide quick or express delivery for businesses?**  
   Yes. Quick and express B2B delivery is available for time-sensitive freight with live tracking and proof of delivery.

5. **What areas do you serve?**  
   Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Oakville, Hamilton, Kitchener–Waterloo, London, Niagara, and expanding Ontario metros.

6. **What vehicles are available?**  
   Sedan, SUV, pickup, cargo van, Sprinter van, 16 ft box truck, and 20 ft box truck (Ontario G licence).

7. **Is proof of delivery included?**  
   Every delivery includes photo proof, digital signature where required, GPS, and shareable tracking links.

8. **How does pricing work?**  
   Quote-based on vehicle class, distance, stops, urgency (same-day vs scheduled), and handling. No hidden platform fees.
