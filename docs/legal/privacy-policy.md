# PorterChain Privacy Policy

**PorterChain Logistics Inc.** ("PorterChain", "we") runs same-day and scheduled delivery in Toronto and within about 150 km. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. It follows Canada's _Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act_ (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), and is written to meet the EU GDPR where it applies.

**Accountable person (privacy):** Ravi Chauhan, Owner (no separate Privacy Officer appointed), privacy@porterchain.com or sales@porterchain.com, [mailing address], Toronto, ON.

## 1. Who this covers

- **Merchants** (businesses that book deliveries, including through our Shopify app) and their staff.
- **Senders and recipients** of parcels, including people whose details a merchant gives us to complete a delivery.
- **Drivers** who deliver for us.
- **Website visitors and prospects** who contact us or join our newsletter.

When a merchant books a delivery for its own customer, the merchant decides why that customer's data is collected. We process it on the merchant's behalf to deliver the parcel (see our Data Processing Agreement for merchants).

## 3. Location tracking of drivers

We collect a driver's location **only while the driver is on shift**. We use it to dispatch work, plan routes, keep drivers safe and show customers an arrival time. Customers see the estimated arrival time and stop status, not a driver's location history. PorterChain can turn live location off for everyone or for one driver. When it is off, the app stops sending location and we delete the current position right away. Location history (breadcrumbs) is deleted after 30 days. See our Electronic Monitoring Policy.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **The driver** assigned to your delivery: only what is needed to complete it.
- **The merchant** that booked the delivery: delivery status and proof of delivery.
- **Service providers** who process data for us under contract: see the Subprocessor List (hosting in Canada on DigitalOcean, sign-in by Clerk, payments by Stripe, email by ZeptoMail, SMS by Twilio, error monitoring by Sentry, and others). Some are in the United States or other countries, so your information may be accessed under those countries' laws. We use contracts (and, for EU data, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect it.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.
- **A buyer** of our business, under the same protections.

Maps and routing run on our own servers (Valhalla and OSRM, based on OpenStreetMap data), so addresses are not sent to a mapping company for routing. Google is used only to look up an address when our own lookup fails, and only if that option is enabled.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask to **see** your personal information, **correct** it, or **withdraw consent**. Where GDPR applies, you can also ask us to **delete** it, **restrict** or **object to** its use, or get it in a **portable** format. Email privacy@porterchain.com. We reply within **30 days**. We will confirm who you are before releasing anything. We cannot delete records the law requires us to keep (for example invoices), but we will remove what we can and tell you what we kept and why.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada** (priv.gc.ca). In the EU, you can complain to your data protection authority (in Austria, the _Datenschutzbehörde_).

## 7. Email and text messages (CASL)

We send delivery updates you need to receive. We send marketing only with your consent. Every marketing message identifies PorterChain, gives our mailing address, and has a working unsubscribe link. We act on unsubscribes within 10 business days, usually immediately.

## 8. Cookies

Our apps use essential cookies to sign you in and keep the service working. We do not use advertising cookies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU), we ask before using any optional cookies, and "Essential only" is the default.

## 9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by role and requires strong sign-in for staff. We log access to sensitive settings. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify you and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as feasible. For EU data, we notify the authority within 72 hours where required. We keep a record of all breaches.

## 10. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

## 11. Changes

We will post updates here and tell merchants about material changes by email.
