# Data Processing Agreement (Merchants)

Between **[Merchant legal name]** ("Merchant", controller) and **PorterChain Logistics Inc.** ("PorterChain", processor). This DPA forms part of the PorterChain Terms of Service / Conditions of Carriage.

## 1. Scope

PorterChain processes personal information of the Merchant's customers and staff only to provide delivery services the Merchant orders: booking, pricing, routing, dispatch, delivery, proof of delivery, delivery notifications, claims, invoicing and support.

**Data subjects:** the Merchant's customers (recipients/senders) and the Merchant's staff users.
**Data:** name, phone, email, addresses, delivery instructions, order and parcel details, proof of delivery (photo, signature, recipient name), and messages.
**Duration:** for as long as the Merchant uses the service, then as set out in section 9.

## 2. Instructions

PorterChain processes the data only on the Merchant's documented instructions. Booking a delivery, using the Shopify app or the API, and the settings the Merchant chooses all count as instructions. PorterChain will tell the Merchant if it believes an instruction breaks the law.

## 3. Confidentiality

Everyone at PorterChain with access, including drivers, is bound by confidentiality. Drivers see only what they need for their stops.

## 4. Security

PorterChain maintains appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

- encryption in transit (TLS);
- role-based access, with strong authentication and step-up for staff;
- audit logs for sensitive settings;
- Canadian hosting (DigitalOcean, Toronto), with an EU hosting option;
- self-hosted routing and maps, so addresses are not shared with mapping companies for routing;
- backups, and limits on how long data is kept;
- live GPS controls and minimal location retention.

## 5. Subprocessors

The Merchant authorises the subprocessors in the attached **Subprocessor List**. PorterChain will give at least 30 days' notice of a new subprocessor. The Merchant may object on reasonable data-protection grounds. PorterChain imposes equivalent data-protection terms on each subprocessor and remains responsible for them.

## 6. Data subject requests

PorterChain will help the Merchant answer access, correction, deletion and other requests, using its admin tools for access export and erasure. If PorterChain receives a request directly, it will pass it on to the Merchant within 5 business days unless the Merchant has asked PorterChain to handle it.

## 7. Breach notification

PorterChain will notify the Merchant without undue delay, and within **48 hours** of becoming aware of a breach affecting the Merchant's data. The notice will describe what happened, the data and people affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken. PorterChain keeps a breach log. This allows the Merchant to meet PIPEDA "real risk of significant harm" reporting and, where applicable, the GDPR 72-hour deadline.

## 8. Assistance and audits

PorterChain will give the Merchant the information reasonably needed to show compliance, and will help with privacy impact assessments. On 30 days' notice and no more than once a year, the Merchant may audit, preferably through PorterChain's written answers and reports.

## 9. Return and deletion

When the service ends, PorterChain will delete or return the Merchant's customer data within 90 days. It keeps only what the law requires (for example invoices, 6 years in Canada and 7 in Austria) and protects it under this DPA until it is deleted.

## 10. International transfers

Data is stored in Canada by default. Some subprocessors are in the USA. For EU personal data, transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914, Module 2 or 3) or an adequacy decision. Canada has a partial adequacy decision covering PIPEDA-regulated commercial organisations.

## 11. Liability and precedence

Liability follows the main agreement. If this DPA conflicts with the main agreement on data protection, this DPA prevails.
