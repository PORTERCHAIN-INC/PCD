# 54 · Support SOPs, staff documentation and training

## Support SOPs (week 8)

| Case                            | SLA              | Steps                                      | Approval                                          |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Where is my order               | 5 min            | tracking link, ETA from model              | none                                              |
| Late delivery                   | 15 min           | contact driver, new ETA, notify merchant   | none                                              |
| Damaged / lost                  | 1 business day   | claim opened, photos, declared value       | refund ≤ $100 auto (L22), above → approval case 1 |
| Refund / credit note            | 1 business day   | single credit-note issuer (A03)            | > $100 approval                                   |
| Cooler excursion                | immediate        | hold load, notify shipper, temp log export | human                                             |
| Complaint / escalation          | 1 business day   | log, respond, root cause                   | human                                             |
| WhatsApp message                | 15 min in hours  | agent drafts, human sends (L27)            | human send                                        |
| Privacy request (access/delete) | 30 days (PIPEDA) | verify identity, export/delete             | human                                             |

## Staff documentation (week 12)

Admin and super-admin handbook: Today screen, margin bands, approvals with signed tokens, WhatsApp inbox, claims, impersonation rules, incident steps, Ubuntu-off mode.

## Training (weeks 12–13)

- **Drivers:** POD photo and signature rules, cooler log, box truck safety and BOL, TDG awareness (refuse DG), distracted-driving voice-only rule, DPWRA rights notice. A short quiz in the driver portal training page; pass is required before the first job.
- **Staff:** a walkthrough plus a tabletop incident drill.
